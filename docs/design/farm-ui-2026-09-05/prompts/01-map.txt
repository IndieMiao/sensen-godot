Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: polished mobile game UI design board, 2048 x 1536 landscape bitmap.
Primary request: design the complete usable map and farm-selection interface for Sensen, a cozy contemporary urban farming game. Create an implementable, carefully composed game UI, with charming craft and exceptional clarity at phone size.
Input images: image 1 is an actual Android screenshot of the existing map with its selected farm, character and farm blueprint. It is a gameplay/content reference, not a layout to copy. Keep the readable Shanghai streets, recognizable player avatar, farm outline and mini blueprint. Do not invent gameplay features.
Composition: pale neutral design-board background, small title SENSEN / MAP. Two complete portrait game viewports at approximately 360:780 aspect ratio, aligned side by side, plus a narrow component-detail column on the right. No phone hardware, perspective mockup, huge title, decorative bubbles or marketing prose. The first viewport shows My Farm focused; the second shows Sample A selected and a ready Visit Farm command. The actual map occupies most of both viewports, continuing behind compact game chrome.
Layout: compact location/status at upper left; a small primary My Farm button upper right. A stable vertical tool rail of familiar outline icons on the right: avatar edit, plus, minus, own-farm focus. Show current zoom as small secondary text. Farm owners appear as fully visible stylized 3D avatars with small farm-blueprint badges and short owner labels anchored to their geographical locations. Use one thin selected-farm boundary. Bottom has one integrated farmer selector and selected-farm preview, not a stack of nested cards. Sample A, Sample B and one other farmer are horizontally browsable; the selected item has a subtle tint and thin outline. Show actual farm thumbnail, tiny crop summary and Visit Farm. No false online status.
Visual language: luminous soft-white matte surfaces (#fcfefa), forest-green primary actions (#287c59), deep green-black ink (#243d35), blue utility accents (#43869f), small golden selection accents (#ecc568), coral only for rejection (#ce705c). Small radius equivalent to 8 logical pixels, restrained fine borders, minimal shadow. Friendly rounded medium-weight typography resembling Nunito, clean Lucide-style 1.5px outlined icons. Avoid all-beige parchment, wood plaques, purple gradients, chunky mobile-game candy buttons and dashboard styling. Distinguish clear action buttons from icon tools. Minimum 44 logical pixel touch areas without visually oversized symbols.
Text inside game, short and legible: Near You, Shanghai, My Farm, Nearby Farmers, Sample A, Sample B, Farmer AA17, Visit Farm, 3 growing, 6 ripe. No instructions inside the game.
Right-side component details only, outside the game: a three-stage diagram for selection and camera focus; compact zoom rail with 15 and 18 endpoint states and a subtle recoil arrow; a farm marker enlarged to show thumbnail/avatar/selected ring. Labels: SELECT, FOCUS, VISIT; ZOOM 15-18; LIMIT REBOUND; FARM MARKER. Use crisp, sparse visual annotations instead of paragraphs.
Constraints: no joystick, no overlapping controls or clipped text, no chat, no new currencies, no real-time presence claims. Both entire game screens fit inside the board with comfortable gutters. This is a design concept, not a promotional landing page.
