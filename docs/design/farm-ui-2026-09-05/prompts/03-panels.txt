Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: coherent family of three portrait game interfaces on a 2048 x 1536 landscape design board.
Primary request: design the Roadside stall, Today's goals and visitor farm views for Sensen, a quiet contemporary urban farming game. Make the controls practical, comfortably readable and visually consistent.
Input images: image 1 is the actual stall, image 2 the actual goals modal, image 3 the actual visitor farm. These are content and workflow references. Preserve the available gameplay and recognizable farm/character, while designing more polished screen composition.
Composition: three complete, equal portrait viewports at 360:780 aspect ratio, aligned in one row with wide clean gutters on a pale neutral board. Small board title SENSEN / EVERYDAY FLOWS. No phone hardware or perspective mockup. Left is Roadside stall, center is Today's goals, right is Visiting Sample A. Use restrained external captions, no instructional paragraphs inside the game screens.
Stall: one compact sheet or modal with soft-white matte surface and upper-right close icon over a dimmed farm. The underlying farm action dock is hidden. Clean unboxed inventory rows contain crop picture, crop name, owned quantity, unit price and clear sale total. A Sell all command follows harvested inventory. Separate Seeds with a thin divider; rows contain crop, seed count, price and a concise Buy 3 command. Keep long inventory bounded in one scroll region. No cards inside the modal, no overlapping panels. Leave some dimmed farm visible above the sheet.
Goals: the identical scrim, close affordance and modal geometry. A single orderly checklist with compact goal text, checked goals subtly muted and the next goal easy to scan. Progress is small, not a hero chart. The dock is hidden. Show 4/7 and readable example goals: Clear the beds, Till the soil, Sow radish, Tend the crops, Harvest, Sell produce, Buy seeds. No bonus mechanics.
Visitor: a fully visible nine-plot farm with recognizable small avatar; back and Visiting Sample A at top, compact Rain status. Only Wave and Stamp commands at bottom, with hand and heart outline icons. The world remains unframed; no edit/harvest buttons, no visitor modal and no messages claiming realtime presence.
Visual system: soft white #fcfefa, deep green-black #243d35, green primary #287c59, fine cool-green borders #d2e2d6. Blue utility, restrained gold totals/selection and coral errors. Nunito-like friendly medium-weight type and Lucide-style thin outlined icons; at most 8 logical pixel corner radius, minimal shadows, at least 44 logical pixel touch targets. Avoid beige parchment, wooden plaques, nested cards, excessive pills and heavy drop shadows. Provide modest colored crop pictograms that look related to the actual farm.
Exact key labels: Roadside stall, Today's goals, Seeds, Radish, Lettuce, Sell all, Buy 3, 4/7, Visiting Sample A, Rain, Wave, Stamp. Keep prices internally consistent: radish unit sale 8, seed cost 4, 6 radishes yield 48; buying 3 seeds costs 12.
Small external footer diagram: modal open hides action dock; close icon, backdrop tap and Android Back all dismiss the modal. Explain through three familiar glyphs and a single return arrow. This annotation is outside the game UI.
Constraints: only one modal per screen, no joystick, no added currencies/monetization/chat, no unreadable tiny text, no overlapping buttons. Complete viewports fit within the board.
