Fill: cream #FBF8F1 when neutral; solid colour only for Ready · Insufficient · Full · Error · New · Event · Gift · Request.
Edge: 1 px, a deeper tone of the fill (cream #8A8070 · green #4F934B · amber #C9962A · grey #B9B5AA). No shadow or gradient.
Glyph: monochrome · 32 px in a 56 badge, 42 px in a 72 · ink #2F3A33 on cream, white on colour, #8A918C on grey.
Rings & arc: state ring 4 px inset 2 (Available, "?") · Deliverable ring 5 px on the rim · progress arc 4 px #5FA85A, shrinks clockwise.
Grey (#DEDBD2 / #8A918C) = wait · done · locked: can't act now, nothing is wrong. Red = hard block only.
Amber serves coins (Sell) and soft blocks (Insufficient · Full); the glyph disambiguates. Badge floats 12 px above its object.

1
≤ 5 map-pin badges on screen. If more qualify, keep the top five by priority > > > > the rest show a plain disc.同屏 ≤5 个地图角标，按 可交付 > 可接委托 > 新内容 > 活动 > 锁定 取前五
2
Durations, never clock times. 0:22 m:ss under an hour · 1h 12m h m from one hour · never 14:35倒计时一律 "m:ss" 或 "h m"，不显示时刻；1 秒节拍
3
One badge per object — the top state only: Ready > Error > Insufficient / Full > Countdown > Cooling > Locked > Done > Available.同一物件只显示一个状态徽标
4
Tap the badge = tap the object. Badge and capsule are part of the object's hit area, never a tooltip.点徽标 = 点物件
5
Not a substitute for the four-line card. Insufficient / Locked still open the card on tap; the badge only says it early.状态图标不替代四件事卡：不足 / 锁定点开仍弹卡
