## Repairs

- Place tabs reset their scroll position when switching views.
- Place action feedback stays outside the scrolling content.
- Decoration content and buttons propagate touch scrolling to their parent.
- Unconfirmed commands are persisted before HTTP submission, scoped to the
  authenticated installation, endpoint, player and farm, and replayed after restart.
- Authentication and farm creation share the command queue; retries retain the
  originating endpoint, token and transport.
- Online sessions with an unresolved command expose a recovery control.
- Normal in-flight plot commands no longer flash the reconnect control.
- Android map calls safely handle the native plugin being disposed before scene cleanup.
- Removed the legacy green exit placeholder from the farm floor; the header remains
  the working map return control.
- Farm camera framing reserves the unlocked seed controls and keeps every unlocked
  bed above them, including the fourth bed. Busy controls no longer shift the camera.
- Decoration pickers share the existing paper/green theme and Lucide symbols,
  with 44-pixel popup rows. Stall seed ownership wraps instead of being truncated.
  Existing approved art covers these repairs; no new generated assets were needed.
- Farm sale recommendations deduct engineering reservations and count only the
  produce that fits the stall's remaining daily budget. At 95/100 with two
  radishes left, the farm returns to its growing status instead of recommending
  a sale that the stall cannot accept.

## Focused Performance Capture

`farm-work.pftrace` was captured during the earlier, ultimately failed
`field-sell-01` run. `farm-work-analysis.md`, `farm-work-analysis.sql`, and
`farm-work-analysis.csv` contain the interpretation, reproducible queries and
raw results.

Although configured for 20 seconds, the 32 MiB ring buffer overwrote
70,639,616 bytes and retained only 6.462 seconds of events. The retained window
contains the end of a resident dialogue, return through the map, and farm stall
interaction; it does not include the later sowing taps. FrameTimeline has 666
system compositor frames with one `Display HAL` jank tag, but only two app TX
window frames. Those two records cannot establish a game-wide jank rate.

Vulkan/Swappy records provide 632 submissions, 631 complete CPU frame samples
and 630 complete GPU frame samples. CPU/GPU frame-time P95 is 5.976/9.487 ms.
The longest submission interval is 669.294 ms, adjacent in wall-clock time to
the place-to-map-to-farm transition. This is a recorded stall; the capture does
not isolate its cause. Submission cadence is not presented FPS, and these
limited debug-build samples do not constitute a performance acceptance pass.
