# C-108 Profile and dressing room in the v3 design (0.4.5)

Source: C-85 pages ① Profile and ② Dressing, design v3 (D-201 / D-202). The design director approved them in V-242 and
the owner in Q-124. LOCK-22 was lifted in C-82#19. C-128 had already wired the pages into the dynamic farm (0.4.4);
this card gives them the new design. English only, and the outfit save format is unchanged.

## Entry from the parcel (① new function 1)

The parcel's top bar now has two rows, like the main HUD (the locked two-row HUD with the location chip under it):

- Row 1: the glass ‹, the **HUD avatar** (C-H1: Ø52, a 4 ring on `hud/ringTrack`, a white Ø44 disc with the saved
  outfit's head rendered in 3D), then Energy and the coins at the right, as before.
- Row 2: the place capsule (the parcel's name over its status), as wide as its lines, 12 in from the left.

The avatar alone did not fit in one row: on a 360 screen the place capsule would have shrunk to about 60 wide. The
guide's card moved under row 2. The map keeps C-128's round profile key.

Tapping the avatar opens the profile over the parcel. The parcel's HUD hides, its touches stop and the player stops
where they are. ‹ and Android Back close the page on top, and the profile last. Settings and the dressing room open
from the profile, as on the map.

## Profile (①-D, the dynamic farm's version)

`scenes/dynamic_farm/parcel_profile_page.gd` (ParcelProfilePage) replaces the P0 page in the dynamic farm. P0's own
profile is untouched.

- **Hero (330 high):** the saved outfit rendered live and large, on a light lime sky. A sideways drag turns it, as
  before. `Edit look ›` is a white pill, 32 high, with the shirt glyph.
- **Top bar:** glass ‹, the `Profile` name tag, a glass gear for Settings.
- **Paper from 300:** the name (`You` without an account). With a home, `My farm` shows the home's card: C-86's home
  thumbnail, the home's name, and the main farm's status line (`Carrots ready to harvest` in deep green, or `Carrots
  ready in 3h 32m`).
  - On the parcel, the card is this parcel, and tapping it closes the profile (you are home).
  - On the map, the card reads `Your Home` and flies the map home (My home).
- **Not shown:** skills, collections and Voice. The dynamic farm has no data for them (① new function 7).
- **Look saved:** after Done in the dressing room, a toast appears under the top bar (white, green bar, 2.5 s).
- **Motion (① 动效 1, 2, 7):**
  - The paper rises 40 and fades in over 260. The figure eases from ×1.04 to ×1 over 320.
  - Scrolling moves the figure at half speed. Past 200, the strong glass goes behind the top bar.
  - The toast drops 12 and fades in over 200, then fades out over 160.

## Dressing room (② A–D)

This is one scene, so P0 and the other packages get the new look as well. ② names `scenes/dressing_room/*` for P0 and
the normal package too. The camera, the figure, the parts, the save (Done → `Avatar.save_outfit`) and the save format
are unchanged.

- **Top bar:** glass ‹, the `Dressing` tag, a glass ↺ Reset, and Done (the primary pill, ≥ 88 × 44). While busy
  (loading, trying a part on, saving), Done reads `• • •` at the same width.
- **Sheet (41 % of the screen, 240–300, plus the gesture bar):**
  - Paper, radius 20, on a 52 `surface/header` band.
  - C-86's hanger stands out over the band.
  - Five Ø44 slot chips use C-86's tab icons. The open chip is white with a lime ring and its icon in colour; the
    others are desaturated and darker (shader, C-I4).
  - The slot's name sits under the band.
- **Cells:** Ø72 round cells, four to a row, each with the rendered thumbnail and its name under it. The worn cell is
  white with a 3 lime ring and its name in deep green ExtraBold. While a part loads, a lime arc turns on its cell.
- **Leaving with changes (② new function):** ‹ or Android Back asks `Save your new look?`, with Save and Discard
  (danger text) and ✕. ✕, the scrim or Back again keep you in the room. With no changes, it leaves at once. There is
  no line of explanation (V-241 ③).
- **Failures:** a toast appears under the top bar for 4 s; a tap takes it away.
  - `Couldn't save. Try again.` uses the red bar, and Done shakes ±4 three times (M-02).
  - `Couldn't load %s. Previous look kept.` uses the amber bar and stacks under the first toast.
  - A new choice clears both.
- **Motion:**
  - The sheet rises 40 and fades in over 260. The hanger drops in after 80 (M-06).
  - A tab switch slides the grid 16 as it fades in (M-09).
  - A chip or cell selected grows its ring (M-03). Cells press with M-01.
